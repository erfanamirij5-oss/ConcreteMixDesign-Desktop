## Authority

## Edition identity
- Designation: ACI PRC-211.1-22
- Publication year: 2022
- Title: *Selecting Proportions for Normal-Density and High-Density Concrete—Guide*
- ISBN shown by ACI: 978-1-64195-186-9

## Authoritative public evidence captured for Gate 02A
ACI's official product/preview metadata confirms:

- scope includes normal-density concrete, with or without chemical admixtures and supplementary cementitious materials;
- the procedure uses absolute volumes occupied by mixture constituents;
- the procedure considers aggregate gradation, workability, strength and durability;
- trial-batch adjustment is part of the guide;
- Chapter 5 is the proportion-selection procedure;
- Chapter 8 is trial batching;
- Appendix B addresses high-density concrete.
